## Approved Models
Only enterprise-hosted models from Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini) are approved for production workloads. Public consumer chat interfaces and unapproved third-party APIs are prohibited.

## Restricted Data
Customer PII (names, emails, phone numbers, SSNs), payment card data, and medical records must never be sent to external LLM endpoints without written Data Protection Officer (DPO) sign-off.

## Ownership and Access
Every production AI system must have a named engineering manager or director as owner. Access must be restricted to named internal teams; broad "all-company" or external contractor access is not permitted.
